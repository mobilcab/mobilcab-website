Mobilcab is cloud CAD for furniture in the browser, on a laptop: design in 3D and get the cut list, with no install. We ship in stages — what’s ready is useful now.

## The stages, briefly

- **M1 — Cut list:** 3D design + parts list *(now, free)*
- **M2 — Nesting, labels, quotes:** when subscriptions open
- **M3 — CNC export**
- **M4 — Team and sharing**
- **M5 — AI + renders**

Details and status for each stage are on the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap/).

## Start free

The foundation (design + cut lists) is available now, with no subscription. Open the app on a laptop and work on your own projects.

## Founding Partner

Joining the program is free and doesn’t oblige you to pay. When subscriptions open, Founding Partners get 20% off full price for 6 months from first payment. Anyone who joins later pays full price.
