Mobilcab is cloud CAD for furniture in the browser, on a laptop: design in 3D and get the cut list, with no install. We ship in stages — what’s ready is useful now.

## The stages, briefly

- **M1 — Cut list:** 3D design + parts list *(now on the Free plan)*
- **M2 — Nesting + labels + quotes:** the Atelier plan, when the stage is ready
- **M3 — CNC export:** the CNC plan
- **M4 — Team and sharing:** the Production plan
- **M5 — AI + renders:** the AI plan

At billing: Free with 3 exports + paid Cut list; higher plans appear on Pricing in order. Details on the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap/) and [Pricing](/en/pricing/).

## Start free

The foundation (design + cut lists) is on the Free plan now, with no subscription. Open the app on a laptop and work on your own projects.

## Founding Partner

Joining is free, only before billing (then enrollment closes), and doesn’t oblige you to pay. Founding Partners get 20% off list for 6 months from first payment on paid plans. Anyone who joins later pays full list.
