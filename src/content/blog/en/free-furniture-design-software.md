If you’re looking for **free furniture design software**, you usually want two things: not to pay thousands of euros up front, and to end up with a **cut list** you can use in the workshop — not just a pretty drawing for the client.

Below are the kinds of options you’ll meet most often. Desktop prices come from the vendors’ public pages (re-check the source); details and a table are in the [CAD comparison](/en/blog/furniture-cad-comparison/).

## 1. Paper, pencil, and Excel

Still a real choice in small workshops: zero licence cost, full control over what you calculate.

The limit shows up when things change. You widen a cabinet and have to redo the affected parts, edging, and the list one by one. A forgotten subtraction means a board cut wrong. The same kind of calculation is in [a 600 mm cabinet on paper vs. in Mobilcab](/en/blog/600mm-cabinet-paper-vs-mobilcab/).

## 2. General free 3D tools (e.g. SketchUp Free)

**SketchUp Free** runs in the browser, with no install. It’s useful as a general 3D modeller and for presentation.

Per Trimble’s docs, **SketchUp Free is not for commercial use** ([SketchUp Free](https://help.sketchup.com/en/admin/sketchup-free)). For a workshop that designs to order, that’s a licence limit, not just a feature gap.

Even as a drawing tool: it isn’t built for cabinet bodies (edging, cut lists, workshop flow). Extensions and production workflows usually sit on paid plans — not Free.

**Bottom line:** fine as a general modeller / presentation tool; weak as production software for furniture, and commercially restricted on the free plan.

## 3. Dedicated furniture software (desktop) — mature, but not “free”

This is where tools like **Polyboard** and **PRO100** sit: they understand cabinets, cut lists, and — on documented packages — CNC export. They’re installed on a PC; they don’t run as a no-install web app.

They aren’t free production options. Examples of **public pricing** (USD, from channels verified in the [comparison](/en/blog/furniture-cad-comparison/)):

- **Polyboard 8:** about **USD $2,795–$5,295**, lifetime licence ([Wood Designer](https://wooddesigner.org/product/polyboard/))
- **PRO100** (Pro100 USA): Pro100 v7 about **USD $3,144.99** one-time; CNC bundles higher on the site ([pricing](https://www.pro100usa.com/pricing-table))

Demo / trial exists on some channels, usually with limits (time or export). The project stays on the workshop PC.

## 4. Online cut optimisers

There are tools (some free) that take a panel list and calculate board layout. They help with waste — **after** the list is already correct.

They don’t replace 3D design and don’t derive parts from a cabinet on their own. If the list is wrong, nesting only optimises the mistake.

## How to choose quickly

1. **Are you allowed to use it commercially?** “Free” ≠ allowed for a workshop.
2. **Do you need a cut list from the model**, or only a 3D drawing?
3. **Do you pay thousands once**, or do you want to start without a large licence?
4. **Do you have CNC now?** Machine export is another product / stage level — not every tool has it, and some keep it on separate packages.
