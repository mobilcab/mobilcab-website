Polyboard is a mature furniture design program, installed on a computer, used by many workshops. This article doesn’t try to make it look weaker than it is.

The question is different: what changes in day-to-day work when you design from the **browser, on a laptop**, with no install?

For a view across more vendors (including public prices), see the [CAD comparison](/en/blog/furniture-cad-comparison/).

## Installed on a PC vs. in the browser

**An installed program** runs on a specific machine. Projects usually live on that PC. The licence is tied to it or to a key.

**A browser program** (like Mobilcab) opens on a laptop, with no install. Projects live in the account (cloud). You don’t run a licence server on the workshop machine.

Mobilcab is designed now for a **laptop**. Tablet and phone come later — we don’t claim them as the main flow.

## 1. Cost: upfront vs. over time

Polyboard is sold as a lifetime licence. In the Wood Designer store, PolyBoard 8 is listed publicly in the **USD $2,795–$5,295** range ([product page](https://wooddesigner.org/product/polyboard/)). You pay once; major updates may cost extra (confirm on the vendor site).

For a workshop that uses it for years, the investment can pay off. For a small one, the hurdle is often the **large upfront payment**, not necessarily the long-term cost.

Browser CAD with a free plan now and subscriptions later changes the decision: you can work on real projects before signing a large licence. On Mobilcab, list RON amounts appear when we open billing — until then on [Pricing](/en/pricing/) you see Free (0 RON) and the Founding Partner path (20%), with no list price.

## 2. Where you work

With an installed program, you design where the computer is. To show the client, you bring them to the workshop or send exports.

With a browser program on a laptop, the same project is in the account: you reopen it on the workshop laptop or another laptop you sign into — no stick and no “version on the old PC”.

## 3. Internet

The installed program has a clear advantage: **it can run offline**, if the workshop has a weak signal.

A browser program needs a connection to open and to save to the cloud. Mobilcab doesn’t promise full offline work; the project saves to the cloud when you’re online. If workshop internet is critical, factor that into the choice.

## 4. Updates

An installed program you update yourself; big versions can cost separately and need a suitable PC.

A browser program updates for everyone. You don’t pick the day of the new version — but you also don’t run an installer on every machine.

## When each is better

**Desktop (e.g. Polyboard)** makes sense if:

- you work almost only on the same workshop PC;
- you clearly need offline;
- you prefer a one-time licence and CNC packaging documented by the vendor (see the [comparison](/en/blog/furniture-cad-comparison/)).

**Browser on a laptop (e.g. Mobilcab)** makes sense if:

- you want to start without a large licence;
- you want projects in the cloud, on a laptop;
- you care about Cut list now (3D + list), while nesting / CNC come in stages on the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap/).
