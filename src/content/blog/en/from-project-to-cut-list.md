Mobilcab runs in the browser, on a laptop. You don’t install a program on a workshop PC and you don’t need a licence server. On the current stage (Cut list) you work with 3D design and the parts list — the cut list you use for cutting.

Below are the steps, in the order you do them. It isn’t a stopwatch and it isn’t a speed promise: it’s the documented flow on the site for what’s available now.

## 1. Open the app

Open the app from the browser, on a laptop. The account is free on the current foundation (design and cut lists) until we open subscriptions.

## 2. Design in 3D

Set the cabinet sizes, place the panels, add doors or drawers the way you work. When you change a size, parts that depend on it update in the project — you don’t rebuild the list on paper part by part.

## 3. Open the cut list

From the same project you pull the cut list: parts, sizes, quantities, and materials. That’s the workshop foundation we ship first on the Roadmap (M1), before nesting, labels, quotes, or CNC export.

![Cut list in Mobilcab — parts, sizes, quantities, and materials](/blog/corp-600/lista-debitare.png)

*Same session: cut list generated from the project.*

## 4. Save and, if needed, export

The project stays in the cloud; you can return to it from the laptop. You can export the list (CSV) or a local project (`.mcab`) when you need to. Right now exports on Free have no download limit; Free limits appear when we open subscriptions.

## What’s not in this flow yet

Board nesting, labels, client quotes, and CNC export are later stages on the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap/). You don’t wait for them to design and pull a cut list now.

## In short

Browser → 3D project → cut list → save / export. No install. Checking before cutting stays yours — the app calculates what you set in the project.
