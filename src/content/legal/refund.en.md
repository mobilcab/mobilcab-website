This policy applies **only once paid plans are sold through Paddle**. Access by access code is free; nothing is refunded for it, because nothing is charged.

Prices, plan names and any offers at billing launch appear on the **Pricing** page and at checkout, not in this text.

## 1. Who issues the refund

**Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders.** Refunds are made by Paddle, not by Mobilcab directly. We don't send money back to your account ourselves.

You can also read [Paddle's refund policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy).

## 2. The 14-day period

You have **14 days** from the payment date (for a subscription: from the first payment, or from a yearly renewal, where applicable law says so) to ask for the amount you paid back, if you change your mind.

If you are a **consumer** in the EU/EEA/United Kingdom, this period covers the statutory right of withdrawal. The right does not apply if the digital service has started, with your express agreement, before those 14 days end.

Asking within this window is not an automatic approval outside your legal rights: Paddle may review the case. We don't use language such as "all sales are final" or "no refunds".

## 3. After the 14 days

After the 14 days:

- **monthly subscription:** cancelling stops the next renewal; the month already paid is not refunded;
- **yearly subscription:** cancelling stops the renewal; the unused part of the year is **not** refunded pro rata.

This does not take away rights for a service that is not as described, faulty, or not fit for purpose, or any other rights the law gives you as a consumer.

## 4. What we don't refund

We don't refund, outside the legal rights above, where:

- there is evidence of fraud, refund abuse or other manipulative behaviour;
- the payment method no longer allows a refund (card or PayPal limits, typically after 120–179 days);
- the request is about free access by access code.

If you receive a refund, paid access to that product ends.

## 5. How to request a refund

1. Write to **contact@mobilcab.com**, with the email of the account and, if you have it, the Paddle order reference; or
2. Request the refund from Paddle, through [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

Paddle sends the money back to the same payment method: typically 3–5 working days for cards, up to 48 hours for PayPal.
