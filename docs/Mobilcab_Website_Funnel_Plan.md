# Mobilcab — Website Funnel Plan
### Turning visitors into Founding Partners

This plan assembles every feature, offer, and piece of copy already developed in the marketing plan and launch content kit into an actual site structure. Cross-references point to `Mobilcab_Marketing_Plan.md` and `Mobilcab_Launch_Content_Kit.md` throughout. The primary conversion goal across every page is the **Founding Partner signup** — the free tier exists to support that goal, not compete with it.

---

## 1. Funnel Shape

Five stages, matching the diagram above:

1. **Awareness** — Facebook groups, YouTube, SEO (Marketing Plan Sections 5.1, 3c)
2. **Landing page** — bilingual RO/EN, demo videos, pricing
3. **Waitlist signup** — with referral loop (Marketing Plan Section 3c, month -4)
4. **Free or Founding Partner signup** — the fork point where the offer is chosen
5. **Onboarding and conversion** — free exploration → paid upgrade (Section 4a)

The site's job is to move a visitor smoothly through stages 2–4. Stage 5 happens inside the app itself, not the marketing site, but the site sets it up (Section 6 below).

## 3. Page-by-Page Specs

### 3.1 Home / Landing Page

**Structure** (from Launch Content Kit Section 1, unchanged): Hero → Problem → Solution/Features → Demo → Pricing teaser → FAQ teaser → Final CTA.

**What changes for this funnel:** the hero's primary CTA button is no longer generic "Join the beta waitlist" — split into two visible options, with the Founding Partner path visually primary:

- **Primary button:** "Become a Founding Partner" → routes to Section 3.2
- **Secondary link, smaller:** "Or join free" → routes to free signup (Section 3.4)

This makes the ask explicit on first contact, per the request that visitors specifically be invited to become founders — while still leaving the free door open for anyone not ready to commit.

**Demo section:** embeds all three videos from Launch Content Kit Section 2 (design flow, AI-assist, CNC export), not just one — a skeptical trade audience wants to see the whole workflow before trusting the pitch (Marketing Plan Section 3b: "show, don't claim").

**Social proof section (activates once private beta testimonials exist — Section 3c, month -2):** 3–5 real quotes from Founding Workshop testers, with name, workshop, and city — specific and local beats generic. Until testimonials exist, this section is simply absent, not filled with placeholder text.

### 3.2 Founding Partner Program Page

A dedicated page, not just a pricing-table row — this is the primary conversion asset and deserves its own narrative, per Marketing Plan Section 5.4's "Founding Partner, not beta tester" framing.

**Content:**
- Reframe from "beta" to co-creation: "help shape the tool Romanian workshops actually need"
- What's included: locked-in pricing for 12 months, direct input into the roadmap, name credit, invite to a private feedback group, first access to new features
- The specific offer: annual lock-in at **49 RON/month (549 RON/year)** for Basic, or the equivalent Starter/Pro tier at Founding Partner pricing, capped at the first 50–100 signups (Marketing Plan Sections 3a, 3b)
- A visible counter: "37 of 50 Founding Partner spots remaining" — real urgency, not manufactured, since the cap is genuinely enforced
- One paragraph addressing the AI-tier tradeoff honestly: Basic includes a one-time AI trial during onboarding, not ongoing AI-assist (Section 3b) — stated plainly here so nobody feels misled after signing up
- CTA: "Claim your Founding Partner spot" — leads directly to the signup form, not back through general pricing

### 3.3 Pricing Page

Full tier table, all four tiers from the PricingTiers spreadsheet tab, displayed in RON (Marketing Plan Section 3a addendum):

| Tier | Price | AI requests/month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 0 RON | One-time onboarding trial only |
| Basic | 49 RON/month (549 RON/year, Founding Partner) | One-time onboarding trial only |
| Starter | 99 RON/month | 50 |
| Pro | 179 RON/month | 150 |
| Studio | 289 RON/month | 400 |

- Each tier's row links to a short explanation of what it's actually for (Basic = pure CAD/cutlist, no AI; Starter/Pro/Studio = increasing AI-assist headroom), so the AI-cost tiering makes sense to someone comparing rows, not just seeing numbers.
- A visible callout box above the table: "New here? Founding Partner pricing (Section 3.2) locks in Basic or Starter at a discount for life — first 50 spots only." This pulls pricing-page visitors back toward the primary conversion goal rather than letting them settle for a plain signup.
- No long-term contract messaging repeated here too (Marketing Plan Section 3b): "cancel anytime" sits next to every paid tier.

### 3.4 Demo / How It Works Page

Standalone home for the three videos (Launch Content Kit Section 2), each with a short written caption pulling the "aha moment" into text for anyone skimming rather than watching:
1. Design flow — "corner kitchen cabinet in under 10 minutes"
2. AI-assist — "catches a clearance mistake before it hits the saw"
3. CNC/cutlist export — "from design to cutting file, no manual math"

This page exists separately from the home page embed so it can be linked directly from Facebook group posts and YouTube video descriptions (Marketing Plan Section 5.1) — a skeptical carpenter clicking from a group post should land on proof, not a full sales page.

### 3.5 Blog / Resources

Hosts the three SEO posts from Launch Content Kit Section 5, published by month -2 (Marketing Plan Section 3c) so they're indexed before launch traffic arrives:
1. "Program proiectare mobilă gratuit — ce opțiuni ai în 2026"
2. "Cum faci cutlist pentru CNC fără să calculezi manual"
3. "Polyboard vs. alternative cloud-based: ce se schimbă când proiectezi din browser"

Each post ends with a soft CTA to the waitlist or Founding Partner page — not a hard sell, consistent with the "informational first" positioning already established for this content.

### 3.6 FAQ / Support Page

Starts with the placeholder set from Launch Content Kit Section 1.6, expanded with the objections surfaced elsewhere in the plan so they're answered before becoming support tickets:
- Do I need to install anything? (No — runs in browser)
- Does it work on tablet? (Yes)
- Can I export directly for my CNC? (Yes)
- **What happens on unreliable workshop internet?** (Marketing Plan Section 3b — needs an honest, specific answer here, not avoided)
- **Is the AI-assist output guaranteed accurate?** (Marketing Plan Section 4b's liability point belongs here in plain language, not just buried in the ToS — "AI-assist is advisory; always verify dimensions before cutting")
- What's the difference between Basic and the other tiers? (Links to Section 3.3's AI-tier explanation)
- What happens when my Founding Partner year ends? (Answers the renewal question from the retention playbook, Marketing Plan Section 6a, before it becomes a support email)

### 3.7 Legal Pages

- **Privacy Policy** — GDPR-compliant, per Marketing Plan Section 4b.
- **Terms of Service** — includes the AI-assist liability language drafted with a Romanian lawyer (Section 4b): advisory-only AI output, liability cap, and an explicit acknowledgment checkbox at signup — not just a link nobody reads.

## 4. The Conversion Path, Step by Step

1. Visitor arrives from a Facebook group post, YouTube video, or blog search (Stage 1).
2. Lands on the Home page or the Demo page directly, depending on referral source.
3. Sees the demo, understands the core value in under a minute (per the videos being built for exactly this).
4. Faces the fork: **Become a Founding Partner** (primary) or **Join free** (secondary).
5a. **Founding Partner path:** → Section 3.2 → signup form (name, email, workshop name, city) → payment via Stripe (RON, Section 4b) → confirmation email (Launch Content Kit Section 4, adapted for Founding Partner instead of generic waitlist) → app invite.
5b. **Free path:** → simple email signup → confirmation email → app invite with free-tier limits explained upfront.
6. Either path lands in the app's onboarding flow (Section 4a): sample project pre-loaded, contextual hints, no forced tutorial.
7. Activation: first successful cutlist/CNC export (Section 9's core activation metric).
8. Retention mechanics (Section 6a) take over from here — this is where the website's job ends and the product's job begins.

## 5. Funnel Analytics — What to Track on the Site Itself

Beyond the in-app metrics already defined in Marketing Plan Section 9, the website needs its own funnel instrumentation:

| Event | Why |
|---|---|
| Landing page visit → scroll depth to pricing/demo section | Tells you if the page order actually holds attention |
| Click: "Become a Founding Partner" vs. "Join free" | Directly measures whether the primary-CTA framing is working |
| Founding Partner page visit → signup form start → completed signup | Isolates where drop-off happens in the highest-value path |
| Blog post visit → waitlist/Founding Partner click-through rate | Validates whether SEO content is actually converting, not just ranking |
| FAQ page visit before signup vs. after signup | High pre-signup FAQ traffic on the AI-liability or internet-reliability questions signals the objection needs to move earlier in the page flow |

## 6. Build Sequence (mapped onto the 6-month pre-launch runway)

Rather than building the whole site at once, sequence it against Marketing Plan Section 3c:

- **Month -6:** Minimal "coming soon" page with email capture only — not the full site yet (Section 3c).
- **Month -4:** Full Home/Landing page live, waitlist signup with referral mechanic active.
- **Month -3 to -2:** Founding Partner page built and live in time for private beta testimonials (Section 3c, month -2) to populate the social proof section. Demo page goes live once real (not staged) footage exists.
- **Month -2:** Blog posts published for SEO indexing lead time. FAQ page live, fully answering the AI-liability and internet-reliability objections before public traffic arrives.
- **Month -1:** Pricing page finalized with real RON numbers, Legal pages finalized alongside the ToS/lawyer conversation (Section 4b).
- **Month 0 — Launch:** Founding Partner spot counter goes live and starts counting down for real.

This means the Founding Partner page and its urgency mechanic are ready *before* the founder-story Facebook post (Section 3c, month -1) goes out — so the very first click from that post has somewhere real to land.
